# Owner-required trailer buying guideline

Michael explicitly requested this as highly important canonical decision guidance: the system must have eyes on what a trailer actually looks like before recommending it. Incorporate into the existing buying blueprint/guidelines through the current docs route. Preserve active F61 and existing queue; this does not authorize a new parallel feature or implementation worker.

Required rule:
- Open and visually inspect the actual listing photographs before recommending a trailer or selecting like-for-like comparables. Dimensions and sale price alone are insufficient.
- Separate visible facts, seller claims and unknowns. A light-duty/heavy-duty characterization from photographs is provisional.
- Verify capacity using manufacturer documentation and VIN/certification labels with GVWR/GAWR. Never infer safe capacity from wheel size, apparent construction or dimensions.
- Compare construction, verified capacity, condition, gate/ramp, paperwork and all-in cost. A generic 5x8 listing is not automatically comparable.
- Insufficient photos or capacity evidence means needs photographs/inspection; do not label it a good buy.

Minimum requested photo evidence:
Front, rear and both sides; underside rails and crossmembers; full tongue and its connections; axle, springs and hubs; both sides of the deck; tire date and load markings; VIN/capacity label; gate/ramp, coupler, chains and lights.

Reviewed public example: HiBid item 323080435 sold for $575. Photos show a mesh deck, low rails, small wheels, a central tongue and rust/discoloration. Likely light-duty is provisional; capacity and hidden frame condition remain unknown. The sold price remains a valid historical sale datapoint, not a recommendation or like-for-like valuation.

Do not include private budgets or personal valuations. Do not fetch new providers, purchase, bid, contact sellers, change the live UI or expand billing.
