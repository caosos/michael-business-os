# Receipt: E-05, stuck-claim reconciliation (Agent 05)

- **Date:** 2026-10-07
- **Task:** E-05 (READY_QUEUE, lane E)
- **External effects:** none. Dry-run only. The job never sends.

## Built
- **`ActionGateway.reconcile(older_than_seconds=None)`.** It selects claims in `mbos.effector_calls` that are still `executing` past `policy.execution.claim_ttl_seconds` (300 s, data; pinned to 30–86400 s by the schema). For each one it asks the effector **by idempotency key** (`Effector.lookup`, guard-token gated and read-only):
  - **Found, and the record is dry-run:** `effector_finish executed`, budget commit, `executing→executed` with `ACTION_EXECUTED` (`details.reconciled=true`).
  - **Not found:** `effector_finish failed`, budget release, `executing→failed` with `ACTION_FAILED`. There is **no auto-retry**; a new proposal and YES are needed.
  - **The provider cannot answer:** the claim is left untouched and reported as `NEEDS_HUMAN:LOOKUP_FAILED`.
  - **The provider record claims a live effect:** the claim fails, and the record is kept in `details.effector_reported`. L3 PANIC is engaged.
- **Exactly once.** The request row is locked (`FOR UPDATE`), and the claim is re-checked inside the transaction. Lane D's `effector_finish` allows only one `executing→…` transition.
- **Allowed under PANIC,** because it records what already happened and never acts.
- **`DryRunEffector` keeps a simulated provider record** (`deliveries`, keyed by idempotency key). It is provider-level idempotent: a repeat call returns the same record. A real effector must answer `lookup` from the provider, for example by Idempotency-Key or message id.
- **CLI:** `mbos-gov reconcile [--older-than N]`. It exits with code 1 if anything needs a human.

## Verification (FACT)
- 7 E-05 tests pass on PostgreSQL 16 with lane D's 0007. The full suite is 218 tests, and all pass. `check_no_bypass`: PASS.
- **Crash after the send** (the process dies between the effector call and its receipt): the job reconciles to `executed`. The effector was called **once**, and the receipt carries the provider's own record. A later `execute()` returns `duplicate`.
- **Crash before the send:** the job reconciles to `failed`. **Zero** sends happen, during or after, and the reservation is released. A later `execute()` is refused.
- **Two concurrent reconcilers:** exactly one `ACTION_EXECUTED`. The concurrency test was run 3 extra times and passed every time.
- **Fresh claim (under the TTL):** left alone. **Provider lookup times out:** left `executing`, reported NEEDS_HUMAN.
- **Under L3 PANIC:** reconciliation still records the outcome, and nothing is sent.

## Open
- The in-process dry-run provider record does not survive a real process restart. In production, the provider (or a persisted dry-run ledger) answers `lookup`. Until a live effector exists, a stuck dry-run claim after a restart reconciles to `failed` ("not found"). That direction is safe: nothing is re-sent.
- Scheduling is not wired yet. Agent 01 should run `reconcile()` as a DBOS scheduled workflow or at startup (A-03).
