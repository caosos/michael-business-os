Wooley Auctioneers will liquidate the equipment, machinery, vehicles, tools and related assets from the estate of T.J. Bruck through a comprehensive online-only auction.

Leading the auction is a strong lineup of heavy equipment and agricultural machinery, including a John Deere 750 crawler dozer, Caterpillar E70B excavator, Bobcat 325 mini excavator, Thomas 135S skid steer, Ford 5610 tractor with front-end loader, Massey Ferguson 180 tractor, McCormick International tractor, round balers, farm implements, skid-steer attachments and support equipment.

Beyond the heavy equipment, this estate contains an extensive and diverse collection of industrial machinery, machine tools, metalworking equipment, blacksmithing and knife-making equipment, welding and fabrication equipment, woodworking machinery, trailers, project vehicles, commercial-grade reloading equipment, automotive parts, tools and shop supplies.

T.J. Bruck was a skilled equipment operator, farmer, machinist, metalworker, blacksmith, knife maker, mechanic, electrician and plumber with a strong working knowledge of engineering and manufacturing. The assets in this auction reflect decades of hands-on experience across numerous trades and disciplines.

This is an unusually broad equipment auction with opportunities for farmers, contractors, excavation companies, equipment operators, machinists, fabricators, blacksmiths, knife makers, mechanics, woodworkers, collectors and serious home-shop buyers.
