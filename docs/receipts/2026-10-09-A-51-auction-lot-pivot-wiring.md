# Receipt: A-51 auction lot through the spine on lane C's C-32/C-33 model (DRY-RUN)

- **Action:** a B-23 auction lot (fixture) now flows through the spine to a scored Item and card using `mbos_economics.asset_deal` (on the C-32 `auction` cost model). New `src/mbos/auction_lot.py`: the lot's stated terms (premium, tax, pickup, rules link, when the bid was observed) and Michael's or research inputs (owner resale target, pickup and transport cost, hours, days to sell, SOLD and asking comps, repair, parts, paperwork, cash cap) are stored as `research[]` entries `auction:*`. `EconomicsEngineScorer` routes only Items with `auction:*` inputs to the auction model, so every other Item scores as before. It passes the current bid as the hammer, plus bid count, `closes_at`, `hours_left` from the bid's `observed_at`, premium and tax as fractions, pickup, comps (the engine keeps asking comps separate), the owner target and the cash cap (engine default $500, or an `auction:cash_cap_per_buy` override). HOLD maps to MAYBE. The scorecard carries `auction` (all-in cost and breakdown, net, days to cash, profit per day and per labor hour, capital tied up and at risk, cash cap, suggested max bid with its assumptions, the labelled FORECAST, the evidence counts) and the card's existing fields (`derived.cash_tied_up`, `time_to_cash_days`, `net_profit_deterministic`, `walk_away_price` = max bid). `EconomicsEnricher` puts the whole block on the card as `value_add_plan.plan` (INFERENCE).
