**How the facts were verified (FACT about my process).**

## Rules the code enforces (tested)
- **A risk appears only when a make AND a model token** from the entry appear in the listing title (or a lane-B `make_model`). Matching is word-bounded and hyphen-tolerant.
- **Tested negatives:**
  - the GP7500E (verified not recalled)
  - `GP65000` (token boundary)
  - a "Honda GP6500E" (make required)
  - a bare "Rebel" (generic word without the make)
  - wrong category
- **No match means no `model_specific_risks`.** The card shows UNKNOWN and the omission reason is recorded. The unknown Cub Cadet, the concrete saw and the GP7500E all take this path.
- **The listing text is seller-stated**, so every risk ends "(Model taken from the listing text; not verified against the unit.)" and states that remedy status is UNKNOWN.
- **The plan is derived from the deal's own numbers** and the matched entries' hints, never from free text:
  - the parts and materials budget
  - the **parts ceiling**, the highest parts spend at which every hard gate passes and the $/h target is still met (a bisect on the engine)
  - the sold-comp target
  - the trailer cost
- **The plan names the limit that actually binds.**
  - A smoke run showed me a wrong sentence: the recalled Cub Cadet "does not clear your $65/h target". The real blockers are the $800 worst-case loss and the $1,500 cash limit.
  - That was fixed, and a test asserts the wrong limit is never blamed.

## Acceptance evidence (FACT)
- **KB entries cite sources:** every entry has an https primary-source URL (cpsc.gov or the agency copy), a retrieval date and a recall date in its title. Test `test_every_entry_is_sourced_to_a_primary_page` enforces this, and an unsourced entry is rejected on load.
- **`mbos.card.validate_card` is clean** (real card code, Agent 01 @ `c4f0156`) for six cases: recalled Cub Cadet, recalled Generac, GP7500E, Campbell compressor, an unknown zero-turn and a concrete saw. A companion test confirms the lint would reject "Check the compression and look for leaks."
- **No elementary advice anywhere:** the KB and every generated plan are scanned with the card's own patterns plus a stricter ban on "check / inspect / make sure / verify it starts / look for".
- **Suite:** 253 passed with all environments, 243 passed with 10 clean skips by default, and the py3.10 stdlib run is OK.
- A regression I introduced (the new goldens broke the C-15 golden glob) was caught by the suite and fixed.
